# Universes
A universe is a named world. It holds your characters, places, things, groups and notes, the stories set in it, and its own genre leanings and word lists.
## What is in one
Entities are plain markdown files with the fields in the frontmatter and your notes in the body, so the folder is also a valid Obsidian vault. Links between entities (a rival, an owner, a parent place, a leader) hold the other entity's id, so a rename never breaks them. Stories live in the universe's stories folder, each with an outline (story.md), notes, settings and a manuscript.
## Making one
Send a Wheel story to the Builder (the Wheel's Send to Builder button, or F2 with a kept story) and choose a new universe or an existing one: you see exactly what will be created first, and obvious duplicates (the same name) are merged. Or press N in the Builder for an empty one.
## Genre leanings and word lists
Universe settings (s in the Builder) set the genres that colour everything rolled in the universe, with optional exclusions and boosts, and a boost for the universe's own names. A universe can also have its own atom lists (a lists folder inside it): Words > Lookup and Genre words can add words to them.
## The generator and your universe
When a universe is ticked in the Wheel, or when you roll inside the Builder, its characters become people in the story, its places become settings and landmarks, and its things become objects; they are boosted so the world's own names show up often but do not crowd everything out. A universe character used as the protagonist is not also drawn as a passing stranger.
## Deleting
Deleting a universe, entity or story always asks first and moves it to the library's .trash folder; nothing is destroyed.
