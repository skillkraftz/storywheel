# Settings
Everything about you and the program in one place: who you are for the manuscript's first page, goals, appearance, the Writer's preferences, spelling and grammar, export, keys, universes, the library folder, updates and your writing stats. Every change is saved the moment you make it, to ~/.storywheel/settings.toml (machine-only settings go to settings.local.toml).
## Keys
### SettingsScreen | Everywhere in Settings
## Mouse
- Click a tab to open it; click a switch or a choice to change it; type in a box and press Enter.
## Tabs
You: your name, legal name, address, email and phone for the first page. Goals: the daily word goal. Appearance: transparent background, colors. Writer: notepad mode, kitty window, font, line height, column width and the other writing room settings. Spelling and Grammar: the spellchecker, autocorrect and the optional grammar checker. Export: how manuscripts are made. Keys: the Writer's shortcuts. Universes: how much more likely a universe's own names are in rolls. Library: where your universes and manuscripts live. Updates: the update remote. Stats: words per day, your streaks and per-story totals. Help: search all of this help.
## Moving around
Tab and Shift+Tab move between boxes; left and right on the tab bar switch tabs; q goes back to where you were.
